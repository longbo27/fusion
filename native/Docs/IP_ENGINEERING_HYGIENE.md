# Public engineering and IP hygiene

This document describes process only. It contains no proposed patent claims, confidential invention disclosures, attorney communications or commercial invention strategy.

- Write independent engineering specifications and maintain dated design/prototype history.
- Do not copy competitor source or reverse-engineer proprietary competitor binaries.
- Organize public prior-art research with technical mechanisms, alternative terminology and industry keywords; verify CPC/IPC classifications before recording them.
- A prior-art search is distinct from a Freedom-To-Operate review. Search coverage, patent-family status and jurisdiction require qualified review.
- Obtain qualified patent counsel before commercial launch and before making patentability, non-infringement, validity or FTO conclusions.
- Protect unpublished invention material and attorney communications. `PrivateResearch/` is ignored and local; Git ignore is not encryption or an access-control guarantee.
- Record public disclosure status and contributors without claiming ownership or legal novelty from an engineering log.
- Features marked `IP_REVIEW_REQUIRED` remain unavailable until a separate authorized review task: computational refocusing, selective post-capture depth-of-field manipulation, focus-bracketing guidance, live/video all-focus fusion, camera-control focus acquisition and depth-from-focus capture automation.

Public-safe Git records are engineering process and already-disclosed implementation history. Confidential feature ledgers, patent analyses and counsel material must remain outside the public repository. The local blank ledger template is not tracked. These gates make no legal conclusions.
